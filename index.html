About Us

Fresh Flowers. Creative Designs.
Memorable Moments.

A Coimbatore studio built on fresh stems

Every bouquet leaves our hands the same day it's cut — designed around your occasion, theme and preferences, from a single hand-tied bunch to a full wedding venue.

Fresh Flower Bouquet Shop Coimbatore specialises in creating beautiful fresh flower bouquets and floral decorations for every special occasion.

From elegant hand-crafted bouquets to complete wedding and event decorations, we create floral arrangements according to your occasion, theme and preferences — so every celebration feels considered, not templated.

💐 Fresh Flower Bouquets Customised for birthdays, anniversaries, proposals, congratulations and celebrations.
💍 Marriage & Wedding Decoration Stage, entrance, mandap and full venue floral arrangements for weddings and receptions.
🛏️ Bed & Room Decoration Fresh-flower bed and room styling for wedding nights and anniversaries.
🎉 Private Event Decoration Birthdays, surprises, proposals and parties, decorated top to bottom in bloom.
